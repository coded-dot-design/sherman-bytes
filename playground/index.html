Ready

Every page is a battlefield.

Drive with WASD or arrows. Aim with the mouse. Click or hold Space to fire. Blast a path through these letters and take the button bases. Pause with P. Press G to find another base. Aim at clear ground and press B to build cover, T for a pillbox, or M for a mine.

Capture the ground.

The enemy emerges from buttons. Disable a base with shellfire, then drive onto the button to claim it. Your captured base repairs armor and fires at enemies. The tiny binary characters left by an explosion are passable ground.

HARD COVER / YOU MUST DRIVE AROUND ME

Supply lines matter.

Fall back to a friendly base when your armor is low. Gather scrap, build your defenses, and survive escalating waves. A cleared wave gives you eight seconds to fortify. Destroyed letters become faint zeros and ones. Your host page keeps its layout and Recall gives it back.
